01 — Introduction Who we are & what we sell Before you can sell Atheris LABS, you need to be able to describe it in one breath — accurately, confidently, without hype. This chapter gives you that.

The studio in one paragraph

Atheris LABS is a software engineering studio. We design, build and ship custom digital products for businesses: high-performance web applications, mobile apps, desktop software and the automation that ties them together. We are not a template shop and we are not a staffing marketplace — every engagement is scoped, engineered and delivered by our own team.

Our engineering core is deliberately modern and narrow: Next.js and Node.js on the application layer, PostgreSQL as the system of record, wrapped in a design practice that treats interface quality as a engineering discipline rather than decoration.

Mission

We exist to give ambitious businesses the software that larger competitors already have — engineered to their actual process, delivered at a size and price they can act on. You sell that mission, one business at a time.

Areas of expertise

These are the four things you will be opening conversations about. Learn to name them plainly — a prospect recognises a studio that knows its own work.

Key pain points

If a prospect describes any of these, you have a live conversation. Mirror their words back before you ever mention a solution.

  • An outdated or slow website that undermines their credibility and loses them enquiries they never see.
  • Manual processes — quoting, scheduling, reporting, data entry — that consume hours a day and invite errors.
  • Disconnected tools that do not talk to each other, forcing staff to retype the same information.
  • No customer-facing portal, so every request arrives by email and nothing has a status.
  • No mobile presence for a business whose customers live on their phones.
  • Losing deals to better-presented competitors with a stronger digital footprint.
  • Growth capped by headcount — the only way to do more is to hire more, and that does not scale.

Qualify before you invest

Green flags — pursue

  • You are speaking to the decision-maker, or can be referred to them
  • They describe a concrete problem, not a vague wish
  • There is a real consequence to doing nothing
  • They can approve a project in the $500–$4,500 band
  • They have launched — or are ready to launch — within weeks

Red flags — disqualify

  • "Just curious" with no problem, budget or timeline
  • Expecting a full internal team on retainer for a one-off fee
  • Insisting on unpaid spec work or free proposals
  • Requests that are illegal, deceptive or against platform policy
  • No authority and no route to anyone who has it
Discovery — five questions that do the work

What is the business problem, and what is it costing you? · What happens today, step by step? · What have you already tried? · If this were solved in six weeks, what would change for the business? · What would a result be worth to you?

Qualifying is not interrogation. Ask, listen, and write it down — a prospect who feels heard is a prospect who agrees to a call with the team.

03 — Commission Structure Progressive & uncapped You are engaged as a commission-based independent contractor. The more you close, the higher your rate — with no ceiling on volume and no cap on earnings.

The barème

Your rate is set by your cumulative number of paid clients. It rises as you deliver, and it never falls.

Tier Paid clients Your commission Standing
T1 0 – 4 10.0 % Entry
T2 5 – 9 12.5 % Accelerator
T3 10 – 19 15.0 % Senior
T4 20 and above 20.0 % Elite
The three commitments that matter

Immediate payout — your commission is released as soon as the client's down payment is received. You do not wait for delivery to finish.
Uncapped — there is no maximum deal value and no maximum number of deals. Tier 4 applies to every deal from your twentieth onward.
Non-retroactive — your rate is locked in at the moment a deal is triggered. Climbing a tier can only ever increase what you earn going forward.

What a deal is worth

Commission is calculated on the full project value. These figures show your earnings at your entry rate and at the elite rate.

Scenario — steady state

Twenty clients at an average project value of $2,250 is $45,000 written. Across a blended 15–20% rate that is $6,750 – $9,000 in commission — and everything above that is yours to keep earning, with no ceiling above it.

How a commission is triggered

  1. Quote accepted The prospect you sourced and qualified receives a formal quote from Atheris LABS.
  2. Down payment received The client's deposit lands. This is the single event that generates commission — not the conversation, not the quote alone.
  3. Rate snapshotted Your tier is recorded permanently against that deal at the rate in force on that day.
  4. Payout released Your commission is settled immediately, and the client moves to active with the delivery team.
Read this before your first deal

Attribution follows the CRM. A deal counts for you when the prospect is recorded under your name and reaches a signed quote with a down payment. Register every prospect you approach on the day you approach them — an unregistered conversation cannot be attributed to you later. Commission terms are fixed by your contractor agreement with Atheris LABS; where this kit and your agreement differ, the agreement governs.

Independent contractor status. You are self-employed. Commission is paid to you as a contractor, and you are responsible for your own tax filings and social contributions in your jurisdiction.

04 — Operational Process Autonomy, with a clear handoff We do not hand you a script. We hand you a market, a price envelope and a team that closes behind you. How you get from zero to a qualified conversation is yours.

Full autonomy

No rigid messaging templates are imposed on you. You choose your own channels, write your own outreach and run your own follow-up cadence. You know your market and your relationships better than any playbook could — we would rather have your voice than a template. We ask for one thing: that everything you send is accurate, professional and unmistakably on behalf of Atheris LABS.

Who owns what

You own

  • Prospecting and building your own target list
  • First contact, in whatever channel you judge best
  • Qualification — fit, budget, authority, timing
  • Discovery and framing the business problem
  • Momentum, follow-up and CRM hygiene
  • The warm handoff to the closing team

Atheris owns

  • Final pricing and the formal commercial proposal
  • Technical scoping and delivery estimation
  • Contracts, terms and signature
  • Engineering, design and quality assurance
  • Client onboarding and the delivery portal
  • Post-sale support and future expansion

Your operating loop

  1. Source Build and refresh your list. Your network, your market, your channels — the list is the work.
  2. Qualify Confirm need, authority, budget band and timing before you invest further. Disqualify quickly and without guilt.
  3. Reach out Your voice, your cadence. Lead with their problem, never with our technology.
  4. Discover Understand the business problem well enough that you could explain it to the delivery team yourself.
  5. Hand off Make a warm introduction to the internal closing team with your notes attached. Stay present, let them close.
  6. Track & iterate Log every touchpoint. Double down on the angle that earned replies; retire the one that did not.

The handoff

When a prospect is qualified and interested, you introduce them to the internal closing team with your discovery notes attached. From that point the commercial conversation — proposal, technical scoping, negotiation, contract — is handled internally, and your attribution stays recorded against the deal. Your job at the handoff is simple: make the introduction warm, specific and timely, and keep the prospect warm until the first internal call happens.

Boundaries — never cross these

Never quote a final price, discount or payment schedule · Never commit to a delivery date or scope on our behalf · Never sign, or imply you can sign, a contract · Never promise a capability the studio has not confirmed · Never speak on behalf of an existing client or reference confidential work without written approval. When in doubt, say you will confirm internally — it costs you nothing and protects the relationship.

05 — Golden Rules Standards, compliance & getting started Your rate is earned on results. Your reputation — and your access to Atheris LABS — is earned on conduct. Both matter, and both are entirely in your hands.

Compliance guidelines

  1. Never misrepresent. You are introducing a real engineering studio. Never invent clients, case studies, capabilities, metrics or affiliations.
  2. Never commit commercially. Share the $500–$4,500 range, never a final figure. Pricing, scope and timelines are validated internally.
  3. Honour opt-outs immediately. If someone asks to stop hearing from you, stop — and record it so they are never contacted again.
  4. Respect anti-spam and privacy law in every market you prospect, including consent requirements, identification of sender and working opt-out.
  5. Handle data responsibly. Prospect data is used for outreach only. Never resell it, share it externally or repurpose it for unrelated campaigns.
  6. Protect confidential information. NDAs, client identities and internal material stay internal — including on social media.
  7. Respect attribution. Never claim, overwrite or poach another SDR's registered prospect. Disputes go to Atheris LABS, never head-to-head.
  8. Acknowledge your status. You act as an independent contractor, not as an employee, officer or authorised signatory of Atheris LABS.
